Questions about work? Ask the handbook.

This page shows what a website looks like with the Docent chat button added. Open it with the round button in the bottom corner and ask a question. It answers only from a collection of openly licensed staff handbooks and UK public guidance on work, and every answer shows the passage it came from.

Try asking
  • How much paid holiday are most workers entitled to each year?
  • How much Statutory Sick Pay can I get per week?
  • When do I have to give my employer a fit note?
  • Can I ask to work flexibly?

Ask something the documents don’t cover, and it says it couldn’t find the answer instead of guessing.

Add it to your own site

Sign in to Docent, make one of your collections public, and copy its snippet from the collection’s settings. It is one line, pasted before </body>. This page uses: 

<script src="https://docent-tan.vercel.app/widget.js"
  data-collection="65af573e-6e3d-4695-8f39-2ae6c4a4e1af" async></script>

The chat opens in its own frame, so your site’s styles don’t change it. Visitors don’t sign in, nothing they ask is linked to them, and questions are rate-limited.
