# Citations are verified by matching a quote against the cited passage

For every Citation, the model must also return the exact quote it relies on. After the answer finishes streaming, the server checks that each quote occurs in the cited passage (tolerating minor differences in whitespace and punctuation); sentences whose quote can't be found are removed and the answer shows how many claims were removed. A second-model check was rejected for the live path as slower and quota-hungry, though a model judge is used offline to measure faithfulness. Holding each sentence until verified was rejected because it delays the first token. The trade-off: quote matching proves the passage contains the words, not that it fully supports the claim; the offline faithfulness eval measures that gap.

**Details settled in implementation (#42, November 2026).** The quotes follow the answer, after a `Quotes:` line, one per Citation; they are never shown to the reader. Quotes pair with Citations in order; a Citation left without a quote of its own may use any quote given for its passage, since models often quote a passage once however often they cite it, but a quote that fails is never rescued by another. Quotes under three words verify nothing. A Claim keeps the Citations that verified and loses the others; only a Claim left with none is removed. An answer in which nothing could be checked isn't shown: the reader is told it couldn't be checked, and the Answer Log records the outcome as `unverified`, apart from Declines.
